STRATEGY OVERVIEW

Cross-sectional alpha at portfolio scale.

The strategy applies proprietary cross-sectional alpha research across a liquid Nasdaq equity universe and converts security-level signals into a broad, risk-controlled market-neutral portfolio.

The research framework supports daily target-weight production and alternative rebalancing schedules. Outputs are available as security-level alpha scores or signed target weights.

IMPLEMENTATION VARIANTS

One alpha framework. Two rebalancing approaches.

Both implementations use the AlphaCatch proprietary cross-sectional research framework and a sector-neutral, 50/50 long/short portfolio construction approach.

01 · DAILY

LSA-NASDAQ-LS

Target weights rebalanced each trading day to reflect daily cross-sectional signals.

Focus: signal responsiveness
02 · EVERY THREE TRADING DAYS

LSA-NASDAQ-RT-LS

Target weights rebalanced every three trading days to reduce trading frequency and turnover.

Focus: execution efficiency

Reduced turnover is a design objective, not a claim of realized transaction-cost savings or superior performance. The implementations have different OOS histories.
